Request deletion of your data

App: Factory of Order (“Фабрика порядка”)
Developer contact: margaritka251289@gmail.com
Platforms: Apple App Store and Google Play

Factory of Order does not use accounts or a developer cloud. Game progress stays only on your device. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob.

How to request deletion (step by step)

  1. Delete on-device game data yourself (fastest):
    • Android: Settings → Apps → Factory of Order → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
    • iOS / iPadOS: delete the app (long-press icon → Remove App). This removes local progress stored on the device.
  2. Or email us a deletion request: write to margaritka251289@gmail.com with subject “Factory of Order — Data deletion request”.
  3. Include: platform (Android / iOS), approximate country/region, and (optional) advertising ID if you know it (Android GAID / iOS IDFA). We do not need a login — there is no account.
  4. We will confirm by email, usually within 30 days, and explain what we can delete ourselves vs what you must clear on the device / via Google tools.

What is deleted

  • Local game progress (scores, levels, robot care state, collection, settings, locally typed display names): deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. We do not keep a cloud copy.
  • Developer-held records: we do not operate a user database. Emails you send to support are used only to handle your request and may be deleted after the request is closed.

What may be retained (and by whom)

  • Google AdMob / Google advertising data (for example device or advertising identifiers, coarse device/app info used to serve ads): processed by Google under Google’s policies. We cannot fully erase Google’s advertising systems on your behalf. You can reset/limit the advertising ID in device settings and review Google’s tools and policies: Google Privacy Policy, ad settings / opt-out.
  • Optional crash diagnostics (Sentry), if enabled in a build: may retain technical error logs for a limited time for stability; they are not used to build a player account profile. Ask us in the same deletion email if you want us to check for any related support notes.

Retention periods

  • On-device progress: kept only while the app is installed (or until you clear storage). Uninstall / clear data removes it.
  • Support emails: kept only as needed to answer you, then removed or minimized.
  • Ad-related data held by Google: retained according to Google’s retention rules, not under a separate developer cloud.
