Common questions

Progress disappeared

Progress is stored only on the device. Uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or switching to a new device usually removes local save data. There is no cloud account to restore from.

Ads / rewards

Some rewards use Google AdMob rewarded ads. Ads need an internet connection. If an ad fails to load, try again later or check your network. Ad content is configured for a child-directed / G-rated experience.

Language

You can switch language in the in-app settings (Russian / English).
