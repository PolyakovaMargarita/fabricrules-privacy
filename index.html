Privacy Policy

App: “Factory of Order” (“Фабрика порядка”).
Platforms: Apple App Store (iOS/iPadOS) and Google Play (Android).
Effective: 14 September 2026. Last updated: 14 September 2026.

Summary: game progress stays on the device only. No account, no cloud. We do not sell data. Ads are served by Google AdMob in a child-directed configuration. Full details below.

2. Scope

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile app on the App Store and Google Play. By using the app, you (or a parent / guardian) acknowledge this policy.

3. Children

The app is designed for children and family use. We do not create accounts, ask for a child’s email, run chats, or store a child profile on our servers — the app has no developer backend.

AdMob requests are configured as child-directed (COPPA-oriented): G-rated ad content and limited personalization. Parents should use Apple Screen Time / Ask to Buy and Google Family Link where appropriate.

4. Data we (the developer) process

We only store data locally on the device:

  • game progress, levels, rewards, settings;
  • companion robot name and related gameplay state;
  • pet names and companion-world state;
  • UI language and tutorial / tip flags;
  • local counters used for gameplay and rewarded-ad limits.

Storage is on-device (SQLite / AsyncStorage and similar). We do not sync it to the cloud and cannot access it remotely.

We do not collect or request:

  • sign-up, login, or passwords;
  • email, phone number, or precise home address;
  • location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library;
  • payment details in our own code (we do not implement developer IAP checkout).

5. Advertising and third parties (Google AdMob)

The app may show rewarded ads via the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. To serve ads, the SDK contacts Google. Depending on OS, device settings, and consent, Google may process, for example:

  • device / advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS if permitted; GAID on Android);
  • device and app info (model, OS, app version, language);
  • IP address and approximate network info;
  • diagnostics and ad impression / interaction events;
  • consent-related information (UMP) where required.

Google processes this as an independent ads provider under its policies. We do not sell personal data and do not send game progress to ad networks.

Google documents:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for ads
  • AdMob and privacy

6. Consent and tracking

Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), the SDK may show a Google UMP consent form before ads are requested.

On iOS, the system may show an App Tracking Transparency prompt if needed by the SDK/OS. The app is configured for child-directed ad content and is not intended for the developer to build an adult advertising profile.

You can limit ad personalization in device settings (iOS Privacy & Security; Android Google/ads settings) and with platform family tools.

7. Device permissions

  • Internet — to load ads and run the ads SDK.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — to serve ads via Google.
  • Microphone, camera, location, photos — not used.

8. Sharing and “sale”

We do not sell personal data. Third-party sharing is limited to what Google’s ads SDK needs to serve ads (section 5). We do not upload local game progress to our own servers.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains while the app is installed (or until you clear app data). Uninstalling usually deletes local progress. Data already received by Google for ads is retained under Google’s policies.

10. Security

We use reasonable on-device practices (local storage, no cloud account). No method is perfectly secure — protect the device with a passcode / biometrics.

11. International processing

Google may process data on servers in various countries. See Google’s policy. We do not host user data on developer servers.

12. Your and parents’ rights

  • uninstall the app / clear app data;
  • limit the advertising ID and personalization in OS settings;
  • email us about this policy;
  • where local law grants access/deletion rights, exercise them to the extent applicable to data we actually hold (on-device with you / via Google for ads).
